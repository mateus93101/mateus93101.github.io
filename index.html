All your email, in one calm place

Mailnest is a desktop email client for Windows. It brings all of your email accounts into one unified inbox and keeps your chat, calendar and to-do apps right next to your mail.

Unified inbox

Gmail, Outlook.com, Microsoft 365 and any IMAP account in one list, each marked with its own color.

Sign in with Google

Connect Gmail through Google's own sign-in page. Mailnest never sees or stores your Google password.

Read, write and organise

Reply, forward, attach files, search, star, archive, snooze and move mail between folders.

Apps beside your mail

WhatsApp, Google Calendar, Slack, Todoist and more, docked in a sidebar next to your inbox.

Private by design

Your mail goes straight between your computer and your email provider. There is no Mailnest server in between.

Fast and offline-ready

New mail arrives in seconds, and recent messages stay readable even when you are offline.

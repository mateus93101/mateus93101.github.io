Privacy policy

Effective date: 12 October 2026

Mailnest is a desktop email application for Windows. This policy explains what information Mailnest accesses, how it is used and stored, and the choices you have. The short version: your email stays between your computer and your email provider. Mailnest has no servers of its own, and we never receive, see or store your email or your account data.

Information Mailnest accesses

When you add an email account, Mailnest accesses only what it needs to work as your email client:

  • Your email address and name, to show which account is which and to put your name on mail you send.
  • Your email messages and folders: message contents, attachments, senders, recipients, subjects, dates and read/starred status. This is so Mailnest can show, search, send, reply to, organise and delete your mail when you ask it to.
  • Sign-in credentials. If you connect with Google or Microsoft, Mailnest receives an access token and a refresh token from them. You type your password only on Google's or Microsoft's own sign-in page, and Mailnest never sees it. If you connect another provider over IMAP, you enter that account's password or app password into Mailnest.

Google user data

When you choose Continue with Google, Mailnest requests these permissions:

  • https://mail.google.com/ lets Mailnest read, send, organise and delete Gmail messages over IMAP and SMTP, the standard email protocols it uses. Gmail only allows IMAP and SMTP access with this permission.
  • openid, email and profile tell Mailnest which Google account you signed in with and your name.

Mailnest uses Google user data only to provide the email features you use in the app:

  • displaying your mail;
  • sending messages you write;
  • searching your mail;
  • notifying you of new messages;
  • carrying out the actions you choose, such as archive, delete, star, mark as read or move.

Google user data is not:

  • sent to any server operated by Mailnest;
  • sold;
  • used for advertising;
  • used to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models;
  • shared with or transferred to any third party.

No person reads your data, because it never leaves your computer except to go back to Google.

Mailnest's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Where your information is stored

Everything is stored only on your own computer, in Mailnest's data folder (%APPDATA%\Mailnest). That includes:

  • account settings;
  • a local cache of message lists;
  • copies of the messages you have opened, so they load quickly and can be read offline;
  • your preferences.

Passwords and sign-in tokens are encrypted with Windows' built-in data protection, which ties them to your Windows user account. Mailnest talks to your email provider (for example Google's imap.gmail.com and smtp.gmail.com) over encrypted connections. It sends no information anywhere else.

Information Mailnest does not collect

  • No analytics, telemetry or usage tracking.
  • No advertising identifiers.
  • No crash reports sent anywhere.
  • No account on our side: you don't sign up with us, and we hold no user database.

Remote content and sidebar apps

Images in emails that are hosted on the web are blocked by default, because they can tell the sender that you opened a message. If you click Show images or turn on automatic image loading, those images are downloaded from the sender's servers.

Optional sidebar apps, such as WhatsApp, Google Calendar or Slack, are those companies' own websites shown inside Mailnest. What you do in them is covered by their own privacy policies. Mailnest does not read data from them.

Keeping or deleting your data

  • Remove an account (Account settings → Remove account): Mailnest deletes that account's stored sign-in token or password and all of its cached mail from your computer. Mail on your provider's servers is not affected.
  • Revoke access: you can withdraw Mailnest's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Uninstall: uninstall Mailnest and delete the %APPDATA%\Mailnest folder to remove everything it stored.

Children

Mailnest is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update it on this page and change the effective date above.
